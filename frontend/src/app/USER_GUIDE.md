# The Charter - User Guide

## Welcome to The Charter! 🌍

The Charter is a visual platform that makes photojournalism accessible to social science students and the general public. It connects photographers with audiences interested in visual storytelling about our world.

---

## Quick Start

### 1. **Browse the Map** (Default View)
When you first load The Charter, you'll see an interactive world map with colored pins representing photo projects.

**How to use:**
- **Click any pin** on the map to view that project's details
- **Hover over pins** to see a quick preview
- Pin colors indicate categories:
  - 🟢 Green = Wildlife-Human Interaction
  - 🟣 Purple = Anthropology
  - ⚫ Gray = Urban Life  
  - 🔵 Blue = Islands
  - 🔴 Red = News

### 2. **Filter Projects**
Find exactly what you're looking for using the filter bar below the header.

**Available Filters:**
- **Categories**: Click any category button to filter
  - Wildlife-Human Interaction (ecology, global warming)
  - Anthropology (traditions, cosmovisions, magic)
  - Urban Life (neighborhoods, sociology, black & white)
  - Islands (exotic locations)
  - News (current events in mainstream media)
  
- **Date**: Use the calendar picker to find projects from specific dates
  - Click "Clear date" to remove the date filter

- **Search**: Type keywords in the search bar to find:
  - Project titles
  - Photographer names
  - Locations
  - Keywords
  - Descriptions

**Pro Tip:** Combine filters! For example, search "climate" + filter by "Islands" to find climate-related island projects.

### 3. **View Project Details**
Click on any project card or map pin to see the full photo essay.

**What you'll see:**
- High-quality project image
- Complete description
- Location and date
- All keywords
- Photographer bio
- Link to photographer's personal website

**Actions Available:**
- **View Similar**: Find more projects in the same category
- **Contact Photographer**: Opens your email to reach out
- **Visit Website**: Go to the photographer's portfolio

### 4. **Join the Community**
Click "Community" in the header to access the forum and networking features.

**Community Features:**

#### Forum
- **Browse Posts**: See discussions about equipment, mentorship, and projects
- **Search**: Find specific topics or questions
- **Filter by Category**:
  - Equipment (camera gear, lenses, etc.)
  - Project Inquiry (ethics, techniques, advice)
  - Mentorship (seeking or offering guidance)
  - General (everything else)

- **View Threads**: Click "View Thread" to read full discussions
- **Create Posts**: Click "New Post" to start a discussion
- **Reply**: Add your thoughts to existing threads

#### Mentorship
- **Find a Mentor**: Connect with experienced photographers for guidance
- **Become a Mentor**: Share your expertise with emerging photographers

## For Different Audiences

### 📚 Social Science Students
**You're here to:** Find visual content for your studies

**Best Features for You:**
1. Filter by your area of interest (Anthropology, Urban Life, etc.)
2. Use keywords to find relevant topics
3. Access high-quality visual research in 10-image format
4. Connect with researchers in the Community

**Use Case Example:**
- Studying urban gentrification? Filter by "Urban Life"
- Writing about indigenous cultures? Try "Anthropology"
- Need visuals for a presentation? Download projects by date

### 🌐 General Public  
**You're here to:** Discover visual news and stories

**Best Features for You:**
1. **News** category for current events
2. **Islands** for exotic travel and curiosity
3. Visual storytelling that's more engaging than text
4. Less time needed - understand topics through 10 images

**Use Case Example:**
- Tired of text-heavy news? Check the News category
- Want to learn about the world? Browse the map
- Curious about traditions? Explore Anthropology projects

### 📸 Photojournalists
**You're here to:** Showcase work and network

**Best Features for You:**
1. Link to your personal website from every project
2. Forum for discussing equipment and techniques
3. Mentorship opportunities (give or receive)
4. Network with visual researchers
5. Get your work discovered by students and public

**Use Case Example:**
- Share camera tips in Equipment forum
- Offer mentorship to students
- Connect with researchers for collaborations
- Get feedback on your projects

## Tips & Best Practices

### Finding Projects
1. **Start broad, then narrow**: Begin with "All Projects" then add filters
2. **Use keywords wisely**: Try single words like "climate" or "tradition"
3. **Explore the map**: Sometimes browsing visually reveals unexpected gems
4. **Check recent dates**: Use date filter for the latest projects

### Community Engagement
1. **Be respectful**: This is a professional community
2. **Be specific**: When asking questions, provide context
3. **Share knowledge**: If you have expertise, help others
4. **Network actively**: Use the forum to make connections

### For Researchers & Students
1. **Cite properly**: Always credit photographers when using projects in your work
2. **Contact photographers**: They often have more images or context to share
3. **Join discussions**: The forum is great for finding collaborators
4. **Stay updated**: Visit regularly as new projects are added weekly

## Troubleshooting

**No projects showing?**
- Check if you have too many filters active
- Click "Clear all filters" to reset
- Make sure your search isn't too specific

**Map pins not clickable?**
- Try zooming in/out on the map
- On mobile, try tapping more precisely
- Refresh the page if issues persist

**Can't find the Community section?**
- Click "Community" in the header
- On mobile, open the menu (≡) first
- Use keyboard shortcut: Alt + C
